# May 2026 source links

All 38 votes have links in columns M–P of `Votes/AllVotes_categorized.xlsx`. The first links are article reports and relevant motions. The last link is the official results compilation, which includes the annual and special meetings.

## Review notes

- Sources were matched using the town archive and the motion locations listed in the official results compilation. All linked URLs opened as PDFs through web retrieval.
- Special Town Meeting articles use their own reports. The joint STM debate vote links both reports. The Articles 21/22 consent vote links both reports and the Article 22 supplement.
- Procedural votes use article context; a report does not necessarily document the spoken procedural motion.
- The town archive calls the revised Article 25 document Supplement 3, but the PDF itself is headed Supplement 2. This is the revised document linked below.
- Some official final totals differ from the existing workbook. For example, Article 1 is 241/8/8 in the results compilation and 240/2/4 in the workbook (YES/NO/ABSTAIN). Vote totals, titles, descriptions, IDs, and categories were not changed.
- These are local changes. The live page reads GitHub data, so it will not show these links until the workbook is published.
